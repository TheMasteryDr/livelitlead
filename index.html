🔥 Our Foundation & Calling

The Three Lit Pillars

Everything we teach centers around these three transformative dimensions of spiritual maturity.

We Are On Fire

LIVE LIT

Intimacy, Holy Fire & Personal Devotion

Living Lit is the heartbeat of your spiritual walk. It is the secret place where you commune with God, stoke the flame of holy passion, and refuse to let the fire on your altar burn out.

  • Daily altar prayer and secret place intimacy
  • Communion with the Holy Spirit & discernment
  • Spiritual sensitivity and unquenchable zeal
We Are Built Different

BUILD LIT

Discipleship, Truth & Mental Fortitude

Building Lit is about structural spiritual integrity. We forge deep biblical doctrine, renew the mind against cultural conformity, and build emotional resilience that stands firm in any crisis.

  • Sound biblical doctrine and systematic discipleship
  • Mind renewal (Romans 12:2) & identity in Christ
  • Character forged through discipline and trials
Sent to Influence

LEAD LIT

Marketplace Leadership & Cultural Dominion

Leading Lit is your external commission. As a city set on a hill, you are anointed to lead with excellence, integrity, and boldness in the marketplace, tech, education, business, and ministry.

  • Marketplace apostleship & professional mastery
  • Uncompromising courage in hostile environments
  • Discipling culture and pioneering Kingdom solutions
